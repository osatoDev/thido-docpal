# Recommended Google Ads keywords for Thido

Start with **ai document generator, ai document writer, ai document editor, and ai for documents**. These combine understandable search wording with Thido's actual workflow: create a draft from a brief, then edit it with AI inside the document.

This is an English-language shortlist for a small acquisition campaign, prepared September 29, 2026. The ranking reflects product fit and plausible search intent, not measured search-volume rankings. Target country and Keyword Planner data were not supplied. Your earlier broad-match impressions do not establish how often people searched those exact words.

| Priority | Keyword | Why it belongs | Evidence or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **ai document generator** | Clear tool-seeking wording for turning an idea or brief into a document. Best fit for the creation flow. | New test; volume and acquisition performance unverified. |
| 2 | **ai document writer** | Explicitly asks for help writing a document, reducing ambiguity with document processing. | New test; strong semantic fit. |
| 3 | **ai document editor** | Matches Thido's ability to revise text inside an editable document. | Established product-category wording; some users may expect advanced file-layout editing. |
| 4 | **ai for documents** | Natural broad category wording with an encouraging signal in your own campaign. | 35 clicks, 6.03% CTR, €0.10 CPC in the September report; no verified conversion result. |
| 5 | **create a document with ai** | Natural action-oriented wording that describes the onboarding outcome. | New test; may also attract tutorial intent. |
| 6 | **ai document creator** | Familiar “creator” alternative for people seeking a tool. | New test; may overlap heavily with “generator.” |
| 7 | **edit documents with ai** | Describes the editing task directly. | New test; use an ad that demonstrates text editing. |
| 8 | **ai writing assistant** | Familiar broader category that can introduce Thido to people seeking writing help. | Exploratory only: may attract email, grammar, browser-extension, or fiction needs. |

**For the first test, use the first four.** Expand into the remaining terms after checking search terms and verified signups. A short initial list makes a small budget easier to interpret. The existing `ai document` keyword can remain a small comparison test, but it is less explicit than “writer” or “generator” and previously absorbed 72.4% of listed-keyword spending.

Current product pages use [AI document editor](https://www.hyperwriteai.com/ai-document-editor), and Microsoft describes [AI rewriting inside documents](https://word.cloud.microsoft/create/en/ai-rewriter/?wdOrigin=APPHOME-WEB.DIRECT%2CAPPHOME-WEB.UNAUTH). These support the vocabulary and task fit; their existence does **not** establish search volume or expected performance for Thido.

## Ready-to-paste starting list

Use these as **phrase-match** keywords for the initial controlled test. This is a recommendation for this campaign, not a claim that phrase match always outperforms broad match. Inspect the actual queries it attracts.

Document creation ad group:

```text
"ai document generator"
"ai document writer"
"ai for documents"
```

Document editing ad group:

```text
"ai document editor"
```

Creation ads should promise a draft from a short brief and an editable result. Editing ads should demonstrate improving or rewriting text inside a document. Send each to a page that visibly supports that promise. Avoid spending equally across every proposed synonym merely to make the keyword list larger.

## Useful second-stage tests

Concrete document types can express a clearer need than generic “documents.” Test **ai report writer**, **ai proposal generator**, and **ai letter writer** separately, after verifying representative outputs and giving each an appropriate example and landing-page message. These are candidates, not proven winners or verified specialist capabilities.

Do not start with dozens of document types. Pick the one Thido handles best and compare its cost per verified signup and subsequent document use with the core campaign.

## Terms I would deprioritize

| Term or family | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| ai writer, ai text generator | Broad writing demand, including short answers, marketing copy, and other tasks that may not need a document editor. Consider later for reach. |
| document ai | Ambiguous with document extraction, processing, and developer products. |
| create document, online document editor | Plausible searches, but users may simply want a conventional free word processor. |
| blank pdf, blank document download, how to create a file | Weak fit with the AI-writing value proposition. |
| pdf converter, scan to pdf, pdf compressor | Utility intent that does not describe the main product workflow. |
| ai humanizer, ai detector | Different promised outcomes from document creation and editing. |

Treat irrelevant phrases as negative-keyword candidates only after reviewing actual queries. Do not blanket-exclude “PDF,” “Word,” “template,” or “free”: relevant users may need those formats, starting points, or a free trial. Add “free” variants only when the current offer and landing page deliver what the search implies.

## Confirm demand before scaling

Paste the eight shortlisted terms into Keyword Planner using the intended country and language. Compare monthly searches, suggested alternatives, and bid estimates. Google provides [keyword research and forecasts](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243/use-keyword-planner); its [historical search metrics](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en-AU) depend on the selected location, period, and network. No numeric demand or CPC forecast is claimed here.

Select terms that combine sufficient local demand with the right task, then judge them by verified signup cost and product use.
