# Information needed for the Thido pitch deck

Yes. I can turn the product evidence in this repository and the founder context below into a detailed, slide-by-slide prompt for a pitch-deck AI. I will separate verified facts from assumptions, build a coherent investor narrative, and specify the copy, charts, visuals, speaker notes, and evidence each slide should use.

## What is already known

The repository supplies substantial product and implementation detail, so this does not need to be repeated:

- Thido is an AI-assisted document-creation platform with a Google-Docs-like editor.
- A user can start blank, paste an existing draft, or answer questions and have AI prepare a first draft.
- Users can select text, ask a contextual question or request a change, and continue the conversation in anchored comment threads.
- The product includes rich editing, document sharing and permissions, comments, suggestions, revision history, import/export, document organization, and responsive authoring tools.
- The current launch model reserves up to 100 free beta accounts without requiring a card.
- Stripe Checkout, subscription persistence, webhooks, and the customer portal have been built, but the paid price and entitlements are not yet approved or activated.
- The technical stack and much of the product-security and production-readiness work are documented.

Those facts can establish what Thido is and demonstrate execution. They cannot establish investment claims such as market size, traction, founder advantage, or financial projections.

## Founder information still needed

Please answer what is known and write **unknown** where it is genuinely undecided. Estimates are useful when clearly labeled.

### 1. Deck purpose and audience

- What is the immediate purpose: fundraising, accelerator application, partner pitch, customer sales, or something else?
- If fundraising, what stage and investor profile are you targeting?
- Will the deck be presented live, sent without narration, or both?
- Is there a target slide count or presentation duration?

### 2. Fundraising ask

- How much are you raising, in which currency, and on what instrument if decided?
- When do you want to close the round?
- How many months of runway should it provide?
- How will the money be allocated?
- Which measurable milestones should this round achieve?

### 3. Founder and team

- Founder names, current roles, location, and short biographies.
- The experience, insight, network, or personal story that makes this team unusually suited to build Thido.
- Relevant companies, products, credentials, technical achievements, revenue wins, exits, or communities built.
- Current full-time/part-time status, ownership of key functions, and the first planned hires.

### 4. Customer and initial wedge

- Who is the first specific user: role, company type, industry, geography, and company size?
- Who uses Thido, who chooses it, and who pays?
- Which document workflow is the sharpest entry point?
- What event causes that customer to look for a solution now?
- Who should not be the initial customer?

### 5. Problem evidence

- What do target users do today instead of using Thido?
- How frequent, expensive, slow, risky, or frustrating is that workflow?
- How many customer interviews or observations support the problem?
- Share the strongest anonymized quotes, examples, or before/after stories.
- What did you initially believe that user research later changed?

### 6. Traction and proof

- Launch date and product status: prototype, private beta, public beta, or generally available.
- Sign-ups, invited users, waitlist size, and paying customers.
- Weekly/monthly active users and the definitions used.
- Documents created, AI drafts generated, AI comment actions, shares, or other core usage measures.
- Retention by cohort, frequency of use, activation rate, and recent growth rate.
- Revenue, MRR/ARR, pilots, letters of intent, design partners, or pipeline.
- Testimonials, customer logos approved for use, notable inbound interest, or case studies.
- If pre-traction, the strongest non-usage proof: interviews, prototype tests, commitments, or founder-led demand.

### 7. Product insight and advantage

- What is the one-sentence product promise in the founder's own words?
- What does Thido enable that a normal document editor plus a general AI chat cannot?
- Which part of the workflow produces the clearest “aha” moment?
- What proprietary data, workflow learning, distribution, brand, technology, or network effect could compound into defensibility?
- What should the product become in three to five years if the initial wedge succeeds?

### 8. Market

- Which market category should investors place Thido in?
- What is the initial bottom-up market calculation: number of reachable customers multiplied by expected annual spend?
- What adjacent users or workflows expand the market later?
- Supply the source and date for any external market-size statistic you want used.

### 9. Competition

- Which products and manual approaches do customers actually compare with Thido?
- Why would someone choose Google Docs, Microsoft Word/Copilot, Notion, Grammarly, ChatGPT, or another specialist instead?
- Where has Thido already won or lost, and why?
- Which comparison dimensions matter most to the target customer?

### 10. Business model and economics

- Confirm the intended free and paid plans, price, currency, billing interval, and entitlements. Repository pricing notes are proposals, not approved claims.
- Individual, team, enterprise, usage-based, or hybrid model?
- Expected gross margin and the approximate AI/infrastructure cost of an active customer, if known.
- Any early willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Expected expansion path: more usage, seats, teams, or enterprise controls.

### 11. Go-to-market

- How will the first 10, 100, and 1,000 customers be acquired?
- Which channels have been tested and with what result?
- Is adoption individual-led, team-led, founder-led sales, partnerships, content, community, or product-led sharing?
- Expected sales cycle, conversion rate, CAC, or payback assumptions if known.
- What natural loop causes one user's document to expose Thido to another user?

### 12. Roadmap and milestones

- The three to five highest-priority product or commercial milestones for the next 12–18 months.
- Which current product limitations matter to the pitch and when they will be addressed.
- Dependencies, regulatory requirements, or material technical risks investors should understand.

### 13. Company and financial position

- Legal entity, incorporation jurisdiction/date, and operating location.
- Prior funding, grants, revenue, or founder capital.
- Current monthly burn, cash runway, and headcount.
- High-level three-year revenue/customer assumptions, if the deck should include projections.
- Cap-table facts that should be disclosed, including option pool or material commitments.

### 14. Brand, assets, and disclosure constraints

- Logo and preferred brand files, product screenshots or demo recording, and any founder/team photos.
- Desired visual personality and two or three decks or brands you like.
- Claims, customer names, metrics, or roadmap items that must remain confidential.
- Contact details and the exact closing-slide call to action.

## Minimum answers needed to start

If completing everything at once is too much, send these first:

1. Deck purpose, target investor, funding stage, and desired format.
2. Raise amount, use of funds, and milestones.
3. Founder biographies and founder–problem fit.
4. The first customer segment and primary document workflow.
5. The best evidence that the problem is real.
6. All traction numbers available, including zero or pre-launch status where applicable.
7. The clearest differentiation from Docs/Word plus general-purpose AI.
8. Intended pricing and business model, even if provisional.
9. Initial go-to-market plan.
10. Vision, 12–18 month roadmap, and preferred visual style.
